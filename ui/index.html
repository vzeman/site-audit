How to read: the scatterplot shows topical clustering of every crawled page. Tightly-packed dots = similar content. Pages in the Drift view colored orange/red are furthest from their section centroid — refocus or removal candidates. The Outliers + duplicates view highlights pages flagged as off-topic (orange) or near-duplicate (red ring). The GEO answer-ability score is a coarse 0–10 estimate of how cite-able a page is to AI answer engines (FAQ schema, question-form headings, statistics, citations).
