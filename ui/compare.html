Leaderboard

Side-by-side metrics across domains. Cells are heat-mapped per column: green = best for that metric's "good" direction, red = worst. Click any column header to sort. Switch tabs above to see different metric groups (Topic shape, GEO, Linking, Density, Action plan).

Side-by-side scorecards

Normalized category scores from 0–100. Each metric is scored against the other compared domains only, so this answers: where is each domain ahead or behind in this comparison set?

Biggest side-by-side gaps

Metrics with the largest normalized distance between the leading and trailing domain. Use this as the fastest list of competitive advantages and weaknesses.

Metric matrix

One row per metric and one column per domain. The highlighted cell is the current winner for that metric.

Combined Semantic Scatter

Every domain's pages projected via one shared UMAP (the only fit that makes cross-domain proximity meaningful). Domains that overlap in the same region cover the same topics — direct competitors. A domain that sits as an island far from the others is either covering a unique niche or has off-topic content. Use the chips on the right to toggle individual domains in / out.

Freshness side-by-side

Stacked bars show each domain's freshness buckets as a share of analyzed pages. Missing dates are included because pages without dates cannot be trusted as fresh.

Distribution overlays

Each line is one domain's distribution of the chosen metric across its pages, normalised to share of pages so different-sized sites can be compared honestly. The y-axis is "what share of pages had this value".
